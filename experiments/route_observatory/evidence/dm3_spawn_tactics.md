# dm3 4on4 spawn tactics (layer 3: spawn-conditioned routing)

Transcribed from `dm3_spawn_flowchart.webp` + the owner's "DM3 4on4 spawn flowcharts and notes".
This is the **decision layer**: which route (layer 2) to run from each spawn, conditioned on
mate/enemy state and item-respawn timing. Resource names follow the data (one YA at `YA.box`;
"2nd/3rd YA" = the single YA's respawns).

## Spawn-conditioned openings (from the colorised flowchart)

- **Lifts** → call "LIFTS", drop to **Pent** fast (don't get stuck in corners). `Got Pent?`
  - **yes** → take **mega + GL** (or LG if available); secure the **2nd YA** (esp. if you have no YA);
    Pent-telefrag the first enemy RL is an option; then take the **2nd RL**.
  - **no** → try to steal the mega; if enemy Pent ignores you, go Lifts for **SNG** + a 30s weapon;
    otherwise SG the enemy Pent to strip his YA.
- **YA** → `Mate call LIFTS?` → routes into the Quad / ring+RA split below.
- **SNG** → `Mate spawn next to you?`
  - **yes** → **split Ring+RA and Quad** between the two players (never one player taking both Ring+Quad).
  - **no** → fight to win (telefrag is good); `Did you win?` yes → **Get Ring**; no → RIP.
- **RA-tunnel** → take **center mega + first LG**; then either kill the enemy RL at YA by sneaking in,
  or return to RA-tunnel for the **RA**.
- **RL** → #1: high-bridge → **window → Quad → RA** (risky; works on team-quad). #2: high-bridge →
  **Lifts** (do *not* go YA — you'll be swarmed before the 2nd YA spawns); wait for a mate under lifts,
  survive for armor/mega, prepare for the 2nd Quad.

## Cross-cutting decisions

- `Get Quad` → `Team 1st RL?` yes → `Got YA & enemy got first LG?` yes → drop down and kill the first LG;
  no → RL paths. `Team 1st RL?` no → go YA and kill the first RL.
- `Get RA` → take SNG and the next LG/RL, or guard RA for the first team RL/LG.

## Item-timing levers (from the notes)

- Armors respawn on **20s**, the strong weapons (RL/LG) on **30s** — "one of the 20s armors", "one of
  the 30s weapons/rockets" = pick among same-timer items.
- Rocket fake: pressure one 30s rocket, then switch to the other **~10s before respawn** to catch enemies
  and free space for a teammate on the other rocket.
- Control **Cathedral YA + RL until the second Quad** (Tower/Cathedral = dm3 area names, not extra items).

## Coach-flag believability rubric (mistakes a stand-in must avoid)

One player taking **both** Ring+Quad · Quad-runner lacking armor · Quad producing no kills · RA left
uncontested · poor Pent positioning · Pent player not rocket-jumping to window.
