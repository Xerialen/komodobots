## The reward, in one paragraph

`reward = w_vel·r_vel + w_prog·r_prog + w_phi·r_phi + w_strafe·r_strafe + w_cad·r_cad − w_press·r_press −
w_collide·p_collide − w_time − w_hack·p_hack`. **Velocity+** (`r_vel`) is a human-relative speedup ratio =
along-route speed / the human's speed on the same stretch, soft-saturated above 1 and floored at −1 (so
`ratio>1` = superhuman, bounded for PPO stability; vibrating/orbiting projects to ≤0). **Progress+**
(`r_prog`) is arc-length advance along the human-reference polyline. **Collision−**/**Time−** are new.
`r_phi`/`r_strafe`/`r_press`/`p_hack` are the kept air-strafe mechanism. `w_cad` defaults to **0** (the
believability cadence rhythm is dropped). Defaults are in `reward_onspeed.DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`.

Smoke the loop first (CPU, artifact-free, ~seconds) to confirm the wiring before a real run:
`python -m ml.rl_onspeed --smoke --cpu`.

**Checkpoint selection (aligned with #427).** By DEFAULT the saved checkpoint is the iteration that best
achieved the **#427 reward return** (the `rew~`/`r_vel~` columns in the per-iter log), gated only by the KL
**stability** bound — NOT the old believability score. So the persisted model reflects the trained objective.
The KL-anchor anchors to the believability warmstart; if it caps the superhuman improvement, **raise
`--kl-anchor-ceiling`** for this run. To A/B against the old objective, opt in with
`--select-legacy-believable` (restores the human press/cadence score + the `--select-by-eval-press` screen).

## What to watch (is the reward doing its job?)

- **`r_vel` ratio climbs past 1.0** over training — the policy is going *faster than the human* on the route
  (the whole point). If it plateaus ≤1, the speedup gradient isn't winning; raise `--reward-weight w_vel=` or
  inspect whether collision/time penalties are over-damping.
- **bhop emerges / persists** — air-strafing (not forward-press bulldoze). The kept `r_phi`/`r_strafe`/
  `r_press` should keep press low; if press climbs and speed comes from bulldozing, that's the ROUND-4 failure
  mode — check `r_press` weight.
- **no new reward-hack** — e.g. wall-grinding for some artifact (watch `p_collide`), or spinning (`p_hack`).
  The corner-vibrate hack is closed by construction (route-projection zeroes `r_vel`), but iterate the reward
  + features for any *new* loophole the policy finds (docs/28's reward-hacking discipline).
- **the recorded MVD** on komodolab — eyeball the actual trajectory (memory `headless-visual-self-validation`
  / `/bot-live-watch`). **No "it's working" claim without the linked recording.**
