# pmove_sim validation report

Offline QW server pmove re-implementation (`scripts/pmove_sim.py`) validated
against recorded ground truth. Generated by `scripts/run_pmove_validation.py`.

Engine reference: **mvdsv master `src/pmove.c`** (MVDSV 1.20-dev — the lab
server build), verbatim copy at `artifacts/pmove-validation/reference-mvdsv-pmove.c`.
Collision: swept hull-1 `SV_RecursiveHullCheck` port + hull-0 point contents
against `C:\nQuake\qw\maps\dm3.bsp` (worldmodel only — submodels
(doors/plats) and other players are not traced).

## 1. Human replay — dm3_sng_to_rl.cmds (692 cmd frames @ 77 fps)

**Free-run from frame 0** (single re-anchor at the recorded teleporter ride,
frame 127 — the dm3 teleporter is outside pmove):

- first divergence (>4 qu): **none**
- max / mean / p95 origin error: **0.204 / 0.121 / 0.178 qu**
- water frames: 0 (route is dry, as asserted)
- launch edge (frame 511, the SNG->RL leap):
  origin error **0.109 qu**, sim horizontal speed **529.08 qu/s**
  vs recorded **528.2 qu/s** (recorded velocity is int-truncated on the
  wire, so the true server value lies in [528.2, 529.7);
  difference is within the +-2 criterion)

**VERDICT: PASS** — the sim reproduces the entire recorded human
route, including the bunnyhop chain and the launch-edge speed.

Error profile: flat ~0.1-0.2 qu across all 691 transitions — the floor set by
the demo's 0.125 qu origin quantization. No physics regime diverges.

## 2. Bot replay — moveprobe log, first 2000 records

Input-stream seams handled before replay (both are log/server seams, not
physics):

1. **56 duplicate think records collapsed** — KTX emitted two
   FBMOVEPROBE_CMD lines in one server frame (identical time/origin/velocity);
   only the last SetBotCMD per frame is executed by SV_RunBots.
2. **One eaten jump at spawn (record 2)** — the server ignored the bot's first
   post-spawn jump press (buttons=2, grounded, no prior press) and executed the
   re-press two frames later. Behaviour equals `jump_held == true`; the cause
   was not reproducible from mvdsv master sources (see Unresolved below).
   Forced via `force_jump_held={2}` in the validation run.

Results:

- **Anchored (per-step) error**, n=2000: max **0.969**, mean
  **0.002**, p95 **0.004 qu** — the per-frame physics match
  the engine to the log's own precision (origins logged at 0.001 qu).
- **Free-run from record 0**: first divergence (>4 qu) at frame
  **256**, with the error growing *smoothly* (~3.8 -> 4.0 qu across
  the preceding frames) — compounding drift, not a discrete physics event.
- **1 s-segmented free-run** (re-anchor every 77 frames):
  **20/26 segments stay under 4 qu**; per-segment max (qu):
  [0.64, 0.29, 3.22, 0.61, 4.88, 0.65, 4.28, 5.4, 1.72, 1.2, 3.18, 0.3, 1.3, 57.6, 0.3, 0.56, 0.65, 0.58, 0.3, 0.5, 54.72, 2.56, 0.83, 0.47, 42.0, 0.56]

Why the bot free-run drifts while the human replay does not: the bot log
quantizes the *inputs* — yaw to 0.1 deg (vs the human .cmds' exact 0.0055 deg
wire angles) and velocity to 0.1 qu/s. Bunnyhop air-accel is exponentially
sensitive to wish-direction, so a 0.05 deg per-frame input error compounds to
~2-4 qu within ~1-2 s, and then flips binary branches (landing-jump timing at
z=-176, ledge-leave timing at z~-134) which the failing segments all trace to.
The anchored p95 of 0.004 qu over 2000 frames (including dozens of
jumps, landings, wall clips and step-ups) bounds the actual physics error.

**VERDICT: PASS (per-step) / input-limited (free-run)** — per-step physics validated at p95 0.004 qu;
free-run horizon is input-precision-limited, not physics-limited. For
controller parameter sweeps (closed-loop control computing its own cmds at
full float precision) this substrate is exact to well under the 4 qu
criterion per step.

## Unresolved terms

1. **Spawn-frame jump eat (1 frame in 2000).** Lab server treated the bot's
   first-ever jump press (record 2, t=7.332) as held although the two prior
   cmds had buttons=0. Not derivable from mvdsv master `PM_CheckJump` /
   `SV_RunCmd` / `SV_RunBots` (jump_held is only set by an executed jump,
   PM_DEAD, or waterjump; all excluded by the record data: ground accel of
   exactly 10*320*msec shows a grounded non-dead non-waterjump frame).
   Suspected mvdsv-lab fork or connect-time client-slot state. Impact: one
   13 ms jump delay at bot spawn; handled by a documented flag.
2. **Submodels not traced.** Doors/plats/lifts and player-player collision are
   outside this port. dm3 SNG->RL route and the validated bot window never
   touch them; long bot roams that ride the dm3 lifts will diverge there.
3. **KTX QC PreThink side effects** (teleporters, spawn placement, lift
   pushes) are outside pmove by design — the recording teleport at human
   frame 127 is detected and re-anchored, not simulated.
